Energy yield uncertainty, from the mast to the meter.

WattOdds builds up the uncertainty of a renewable energy project one item at a time, from the measurement on site to the point of interconnection. Use whatever information you have, and get P50, P90 and P99 values you can trace back to every assumption.

What WattOdds does

Works with what you have

For each item, calculate it, describe the site, enter a value from a report or use a cited default. The results show how much of the total rests on generic values.

81 items, each sourced

Instruments, long-term correction, flow models, wakes, soiling, availability, curtailment and more. Each item says what drives it, how to reduce it and where its numbers come from.

P-values for one year, ten years and the plant's life, checked by Monte Carlo. A ranked list shows which items would lift P90 most if you reduced them. Export it all to Excel.
